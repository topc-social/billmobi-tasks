# Billmobi's opening balance (2020–2022)

Billmobi (BillMobi Technologies Pvt Ltd, digital receipts) ran from Feb 2020 to Oct 2022, before this ledger existed. Its opening rows bring in the past work of the people carrying it forward.

## How the old company ended

On 14 Jun 2022 the four shareholders (Kamal, Himanshu Singh, Yash Chamadia, Harsh Khatwani) agreed the terms of dissolution: physical assets and cash valued and paid out by equity stake (done Jul–Aug 2022), code and designs free for all of them to use, and the name Billmobi kept by Kamal. The company was then wound up. That was a final settlement: each shareholder was paid out on their stake.

## The rule

1. **A wound-up venture's old stakes end with its final payout.** So the shareholders' opening rows (3–6, corrected by 10–13) are cancelled by rows 14–16. Nobody's 2020–22 work is denied; it was settled.
2. **Whoever carries the work forward brings in their own past work**, priced on the rate card at pre-2025 hand-work prices, minus any cash they were paid for it. Kamal keeps the name and continues Billmobi: **7,22,562 Neki** (rows 3, 10, 17).
3. **Unpaid work outside the settlement stays owed.** Intesar Sheikh designed Billmobi's brand and app (logo, ~69 app screens in the "Billmobi - Intesar" Figma file, banners, research boards) as a freelancer, wasn't a shareholder and got nothing from the wind-up: **1,30,000 Neki** for the unpaid part (row 9).
4. From here every accepted task adds Neki the normal way; shares are Neki over all Neki.
5. **Provisional.** Anyone named can comment on [#2](https://github.com/topc-social/billmobi-tasks/issues/2).

## What the past work was worth

All Billmobi work 2020–22, priced before cash, came to 21,71,100 Neki: code 15,07,100 (36 shipped features across 31 GitLab and 9 GitHub repos, including the order integrations run for clients), running those integrations and the client deal 1,62,000, decks, programme applications, website, video and writing 2,46,000, shop visits across Pune 51,000, UI design and brand 1,75,000, finance and compliance 30,000.

Sources: GitLab, GitHub, Gmail, Zoho Mail, Google Drive, Trello, Slack, WhatsApp and Zoom recordings from the time. Pay amounts and private messages stay out of this public repo.
